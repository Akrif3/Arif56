Rodri transfer news: Man City reject initial Barcelona offer for Spain midfielder with bid considered far too low

Manchester City have rejected an initial offer from Barcelona for Rodri; the bid is considered far too low for the Spain midfielder; Sky in Italy have reported that Chelsea's Enzo Fernandez would be City boss Enzo Maresca's "dream" signing

Manchester City have knocked back an initial offer from Barcelona for Rodri that is considered far too low. Barcelona's initial offer - which Manchester City rejected - was worth in the region of £40m. City are seeking upwards of £60m for the 30-year-old midfielder, who has entered the final year of his deal.
